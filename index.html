        About MM

        A code-breaking race for two people, wherever they are. There are no accounts, no ads and no saved games: just pegs, logic and a friend.

        Starting a game

        1. Pick a level, then Start a game.
        2. Send the code (or the link) to your friend.
        3. When they join, you each make a secret code.

        Playing

        • Your secret is a row of colored pegs. Colors can repeat.
        • Once you’ve both locked in, you race to crack each other’s code. Tap colors to fill a row, then Check. Keyboard: 1 to 8 for colors, Backspace, Enter.
        • Each guess is scored with small pegs: a filled one for every peg of the right color in the right place, and a ring for every right color in the wrong place. They don’t say which pegs they mean.
        • Crack the code in fewer tries than your opponent to win. Same number of tries, or neither of you cracks it, is a draw.
        • You can watch their guesses at your code as they go.
        • Play again starts a new round at the same level.

        Fair play

        Your code never leaves your browser while the round is on: your browser scores your opponent’s guesses itself. Before guessing starts, each browser sends a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of its code; at the end the codes are revealed and checked against the fingerprints and every score given, so nobody can change their code or fib about a score.

        Privacy

        MM is a static page. A public PeerJS server introduces your two browsers using the game code; after that, guesses, chat, video and audio travel directly between you over WebRTC. Nothing is recorded, and only your settings are saved, in this browser.

        Give up this round?

        Your opponent wins the round, and you both get to see the codes.

        Leave this game?

        The game ends for both of you and can’t be resumed.

        Connection lost

        The link to your opponent dropped.
